    The tools

    What the crew works with

    Six services, each with one job. Every account belongs to Garmentory, so the system keeps working when people change roles.

    the memory

    Airtable

    Every boutique, contact and email sent. Where each boutique is in its sequence.

    the voice

    Gmail

    Drafts are written here and replies arrive here. You send from here.

    the lists

    Google Sheets

    The Do Not Reach Out list, read-only, and the list of designers to search for.

    the address book

    Hunter.io

    Finds the email of an owner or buyer when the website doesn't show one.

    the search bar

    SerpAPI

    Runs Google searches on the researcher's behalf, on a set budget.

    the guest list

    Redash

    Garmentory's own data. Tells the scouts which boutiques are already partners.

    The rules

    Lines it never crosses

    Each rule exists because breaking it once landed in a real boutique's inbox. They're built into the code.

    Do Not Reach Out means never

    Anyone on the DNRO sheet is skipped everywhere. The sheet is read, never written.

    Partners are off-limits

    Live, onboarding and partner boutiques never get a cold email.

    Drafts only

    The system writes. A person reads and sends. It never sends outreach on its own.

    Rehearse first

    Every run is a dry run until you add --live. Read what it plans, then let it act.

    Once written to, locked

    A contact we've emailed keeps their address. New info becomes a new contact, never an overwrite.

    Paid lookups, on purpose

    Hunter is used at three moments only: a new prospect, a second contact, a bounced address.

    Watch out

    Partners on vacation mode can look brand new

    When a partner pauses their store for a few days, the partner list can miss them, so a scout may report them as a new boutique. Before you promote a name you don't recognize, check it isn't already on Garmentory.

    Your day

    About twenty minutes of you

    Start the run, go do something else, come back to drafts and a report. Tick these off as you go.
